Sound Valve Speaker

Patent SE542440C2 · Inventor: Joakim Pettersson

A combined ventilation valve and loudspeaker that controls air pressure, noise and full-spectrum sound through the same opening.

Designed for forced-air systems where conventional noise cancellation performs poorly, especially in the 10–100 Hz range.

Energy-efficient HVAC Low-frequency noise control Embedded & electronics

Engineering Summary

  • Membrane valves replace standard supply/return vents in a room or AC unit.
  • Airflow is modulated in real time to cancel low-frequency noise from DC up to ~2 kHz.
  • Acts as both active silencer and full-spectrum loudspeaker.
  • Reduces perceived noise by roughly 10–15 dB in typical ventilation / AC scenarios.
  • Targets homes, offices, studios and vehicles where comfort and energy efficiency both matter.
